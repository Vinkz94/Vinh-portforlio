About me

I’m an ecommerce operator who enjoys digging into data and turning it into changes that help online stores sell more effectively: sharper listings, better-tuned ads, emails worth opening, and reports that show where customers drop off.

I came to ecommerce from technical support, where I spent years solving storage, data recovery, and networking problems for customers. That habit of finding the real cause and explaining it plainly still shapes how I work with data and with people.
